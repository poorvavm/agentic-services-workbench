# The Challenge

Act as a Technical Architect and prove — with a working prototype, not
just a written strategy — that a unified "post-sales workbench" is viable.
The target problem: post-sales delivery is fragmented across three
disconnected tools (a project-delivery/PSA tool, a customer-success
platform, and a CRM), causing lost context at every handoff and delayed
time-to-value for customers.

**The core ask, broken into three parts:**

1. **A functional prototype**, built with an AI app builder, proving that
   structured fields from a pre-sales handoff document can deterministically
   map to a generated technical requirements doc / execution plan — not
   just describe how that mapping *would* work.
2. **A written architecture strategy** covering integration (bidirectional
   sync without data loops), an AI/RAG layer for a "configuration copilot"
   grounded in current product docs, and a resourcing engine that
   recommends the best-fit person for a job rather than just the first
   available one.
3. **A success/ROI framework** — metrics a business/GTM audience would
   care about (time to deploy, time to value, reduction in delivery
   friction), not model-accuracy metrics.
